          Appearance

          API access

          Keys stay on this device. They're never included in backups, History or drafts, and each is only sent to its own service.

          GeminiCard reading and web price search

          With this key, Gemini reads your card from the front photo (free; the photo is sent to Google, and on Google's free tier it may be used to improve their products). Web search asks Gemini what this card recently sold for, and shows the sources. Get a key at aistudio.google.com. Google only allows web search on keys with billing turned on (5,000 searches a month are then free; each card search uses a few, plus a fraction of a cent). Results aren't saved, as Google's terms require.

          PriceChartingGraded prices (paid subscription)

          Graded prices come from PriceCharting's API, which needs your own paid PriceCharting subscription. Paste the 40-character token from its Subscription page (API/Download). It's only sent to pricecharting.com. PriceCharting licenses this data for your own use.

          CardSightRecent sales, Pokémon

          Recent sales come from CardSight AI with your own API key (free plan: 750 calls a month, no charges; each card lookup uses 2, and answers are kept for a day). It's only sent to CardSight. CardSight's terms don't allow sharing the key.

          Backup

          Saved cards live only in this browser. Copy a backup now and then, and restore it here on this or another device. Photos and keys aren't included.

          About

          Photo checks, centering and grade estimates run on this device. Card lookups and prices only send the card's details (or, with a Gemini key, the front photo) to the service you use. Estimates are theoretical: no grading company is affiliated with this app.

          Card details

          Printed at the bottom of the card.

          Reads the name and number from your front photo, then looks the card up. With a Gemini key saved (Settings), Gemini reads it and the front photo is sent to Google; without one, it's read on this device and only the text is sent.

          Reference

          Support evidence only. A reference card can have its own wear, and its grade is never applied to yours.
