2Centering

Photograph each side straight on, with the card on a dark, plain background. The app finds the card, flattens it and measures the borders. Drag the card-edge and frame guides to fine-tune them. For full-art cards, line the frame guides up with the printed frame or type the ratios.

3Corners, edges & surface

Tap a corner, edge or the middle of the card to log a defect there. Look under a bright light, tilt the card, and use a loupe if you have one.
